Disconnect QuickBooks

This page is where a Corr-Tech user can revoke the CorrTech Bookkeeping app’s access to QuickBooks.

To disconnect: In QuickBooks Online, go to Settings → Connected apps (or Apps), find CorrTech Bookkeeping, and select Disconnect or Revoke access.

You can also revoke access from your Intuit account’s connected-app settings. Disconnecting stops the app from accessing your QuickBooks data.
