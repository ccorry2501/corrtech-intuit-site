Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Corr-Tech, Inc. (“Corr-Tech,” “we,” “us”) handles data through the CorrTech Bookkeeping application (“the App”), an internal bookkeeping integration used exclusively by Corr-Tech.

1. Scope

The App is a private, internal tool. It is not sold or distributed to the public. It connects Corr-Tech’s QuickBooks Online company to Corr-Tech’s internal bookkeeping workflow.

2. Data We Access

With your authorization, the App accesses data in Corr-Tech’s QuickBooks Online company, including invoices, bills, bank deposits, transfers, journal entries, customers, vendors, and related accounting records. Access is limited to what is needed to perform bookkeeping tasks.

3. How Data Is Used

Data is used solely to perform bookkeeping for Corr-Tech: recording journal entries, bank deposits, and transfers; matching bank activity; and generating and managing invoices. Data is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

Access credentials (OAuth tokens) are stored securely and are used only to connect to QuickBooks. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections. Access is restricted to authorized Corr-Tech personnel and its authorized service provider (Lindy).

5. Data Retention

Corr-Tech retains its accounting records in accordance with its business and legal obligations. You may revoke the App’s access to QuickBooks at any time by disconnecting the App.

6. Your Choices

You can disconnect the App from QuickBooks at any time using the Disconnect page. Disconnecting stops further access to your QuickBooks data.

7. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Corr-Tech, Inc., Wheaton, Illinois.
